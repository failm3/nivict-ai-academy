### Gate-specific interpretation

- **G1:** configure native per-delegation ToolSearch only after reading current actual Nox. Prove only-current-grants search/load/active/call, revoke/restart/trimming/concurrency negatives, and measure against equal-task full-tool injection. None of these external candidates substitutes for that proof.
- **G2:** exactly one owner-selected read-only acquisition at a time; pin source/package/digest and independently inspect the call/receipt. Inspector is the cleanest diagnostic candidate; Playwright MCP is conditional because historical unpinned `npx` is not current proof.
- **G3:** begin with authorized immutable source fixtures, stable anchors/rights, held-out fresh-agent or deterministic gold oracles, and rebuildable derived indexes. No universal-parser, memory-replacement, or competence claim.
- **G4:** instrument only a demonstrated metric gap. Compare equal budgets and complete cost state; Langfuse/OpenAI Evals/agent skills are aids, not independent judges or automatic acceptance.

Historical proposal locations to validate only with fresh Nox access: `src/tools/` + index, `src/config/mcp.ts`, `src/security/tool-context.ts`, `src/agents/specialists.ts`, and MM admission/effect/QA paths. No edits are proposed as presently compatible.
